**Allium Explorer** is a one-stop destination to **query, analyze, and visualize** our [Historical Data](/historical-chains/overview). Explorer is a web app that allows you to query, and visualize blockchain data across 80+ blockchains and share your insights through interactive charts.

### Query Engine

The Allium Explorer runs on **Snowflake Data Warehouse**.

### Schema Catalog

The Allium Explorer provides access to all **80+ blockchains** listed on this [page](/historical-chains/supported-blockchains).
