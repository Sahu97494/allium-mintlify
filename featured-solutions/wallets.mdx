Wallets providers use [Developer](/data-products-real-time/allium-developer) and [Datastreams](/data-products-real-time/allium-datastreams) to power their UX and backends with real-time [enriched transaction data](/data-products-real-time/allium-developer/wallet-apis) and [block-level wallet balances](/data-products-real-time/allium-developer/wallet-apis). Wallets [benefit](https://www.allium.so/post/the-inside-story-of-the-jup-airdrop-at-phantom) from Allium's horizontally scalable infrastructure for consistent stability throughout peak network traffic (up to 100K+ QPS).

Wallet analysts use [Explorer](/data-products-analytics/allium-explorer) and [Datashares](/data-products-analytics/allium-datashares) to source user behavior insights and ecosystem intelligence through enriched schemas like [Wallet360](/historical-verticals/wallet-360).
