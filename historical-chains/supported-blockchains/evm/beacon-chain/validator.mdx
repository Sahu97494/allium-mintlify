---
title: "Overview"
---

The validator schema includes daily balance snapshot of validators, validator index (withdrawal address and pubkey) as well as daily validator income.

The validator schema updates daily at \~0300-0400 UTC.

<table><thead><tr><th width="209">Table</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Income</td><td>Daily consensus income of active validators on Beacon Chain derived from balance change, withdrawals and deposits. </td></tr><tr><td>Balances</td><td>Daily snapshot of active validators on Beacon Chain.</td></tr><tr><td>Index</td><td>List of validator by index, pubkey, ETH1 withdrawal address. </td></tr><tr><td>Entities</td><td>Entities labelled based on deposit address or contract address (e.g. Lido)</td></tr></tbody></table>
