### Avalanche Tables

Our Avalanche (C-Chain) data includes the following schemas and tables:

<table><thead><tr><th width="140.00000000000003">Schema</th><th width="340">Description</th><th>Tables</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Raw</td><td>Raw blockchain data.</td><td>blocks, transactions, logs, traces</td></tr><tr><td>Raw+</td><td>Deployed contracts with bytecode and ERC token data.</td><td>contracts, erc20_tokens, erc721_tokens, erc_1155_tokens </td></tr><tr><td>Assets</td><td>Token transfers for native AVAX and ERC tokens. </td><td><p>avax_erc20_token_transfers,</p><p>avax_token_transfers, erc20_token_transfers,</p><p>erc721_token_transfers,</p><p>erc1155_token_transfers,</p></td></tr></tbody></table>

*Our Avalanche data tables are structurally similar to Ethereum across all schemas.*
