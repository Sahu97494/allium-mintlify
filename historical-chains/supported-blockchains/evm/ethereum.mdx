Our Ethereum data model consists of multiple verticals, ranging from raw blockchain data to enriched abstractions that allow users to gain insights about a specific DeFi sector with a simple query.

### Ethereum Data Schema

Our Ethereum data includes the following schemas:

<table><thead><tr><th data-type="content-ref">Schema</th><th>Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/raw/">RAW</a></td><td><em>Blocks, transactions, logs and contracts</em> </td></tr><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/decoded/">decoded</a></td><td><em>Decoded event logs and traces data</em> </td></tr><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/assets/">Assets</a></td><td><em>Historical and current wallet balances, credit debit and transfers</em></td></tr><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/wallet-360">WALLET 360</a></td><td><em>Comprehensive insights to a wallet's behaviour - coverage includes NFT, DEX and transaction data</em></td></tr><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/nfts/">NFTS</a></td><td><em>NFT Mints, trades in popular marketplaces</em></td></tr><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/dex/">DEX</a></td><td><em>DEX trades and liquidity events</em>  </td></tr><tr><td><a href="./ethereum/ens">ENS</a></td><td><em>ENS names, registration, renewals &#x26; transfers</em></td></tr></tbody></table>
