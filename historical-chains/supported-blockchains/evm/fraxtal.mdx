### Fraxtal Tables

Our Fraxtal data includes the following schemas and tables:

<table><thead><tr><th width="140.00000000000003">Schema</th><th width="340">Description</th><th>Tables</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Raw</td><td>Raw blockchain data.</td><td>blocks, transactions, logs, traces</td></tr><tr><td>Raw+</td><td>Deployed contracts with bytecode</td><td>contracts</td></tr></tbody></table>

*Our Fraxtal data tables are structurally similar to Ethereum across all schemas.*
