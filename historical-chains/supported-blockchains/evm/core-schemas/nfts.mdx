Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) represent unique digital assets on the blockchain, enabling verifiable ownership and trading of digital art, collectibles, gaming items, and other unique digital assets.

Allium provides extensive NFT data coverage including minting events, marketplace trades, transfer activity, and wash trading detection, allowing users to analyze NFT market dynamics, trading patterns, and collection performance across the ecosystem.

| Models         | Description                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| NFT Mints      | Enriched data on on-chain NFT mints, including standard and contract-specific mints with minting currency and USD cost |
| NFT Trades     | Aggregate and marketplace-specific trades from marketplaces and protocols indexed by Allium                            |
| NFT With Flags | Curated wash trading scoring based on funding activity and volume to filter out inorganic nft volumes                  |
| NFT Transfers  | Transfers of erc721 and erc1155 tokens.                                                                                |
