Our Base assets schema currently includes ETH and ERC Token Transfers.

<table data-full-width="false"><thead><tr><th width="190.33333333333331">Table</th><th width="559">Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Balances</td><td>Block level balances of ETH and ERC20 tokens on Base. </td></tr><tr><td>Token Transfers</td><td>Records transfers of ETH, ERC20, ERC721 and ERC1155 between addresses.</td></tr></tbody></table>

Each of the categories has both aggregated and asset-specific (ETH, ERC20, ERC721 & ERC1155) tables. For example,`base.assets.balances` provides an aggregate of all assets while `base.assets.eth_balances`containing ETH balances only.
