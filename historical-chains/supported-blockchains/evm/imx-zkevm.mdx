### IMX ZkEVM Tables

Our IMX data includes the following schemas and tables:

<table><thead><tr><th width="140.00000000000003">Schema</th><th width="340">Description</th><th>Tables</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Raw</td><td>Raw blockchain data.</td><td>blocks, transactions, logs, traces</td></tr><tr><td>Raw+</td><td>Deployed contracts with bytecode and ERC token data.</td><td>contracts, tokens</td></tr><tr><td>Assets</td><td>Token transfers for ERC tokens. </td><td><p></p><p>erc20_token_transfers, <br />erc721_token_transfers, erc1155_token_transfers</p></td></tr></tbody></table>
