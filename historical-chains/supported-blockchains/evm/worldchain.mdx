title: "Overview"
---

World Chain is a human-centric blockchain built on the Superchain that offers free gas fees for verified humans, native mobile distribution, and simplified crypto transactions through mini-apps. It aims to make blockchain technology more accessible to everyday users while maintaining security through World ID verification, with additional benefits including WLD token airdrops for verified humans.
