Allium currently supports Solana data from the Genesis block.

Our Solana data schemas include NFTs, DEXs and assets (transfers/credit debit) data. Support for additional verticals and programs can be made available upon request.

<Card href="https://docs.solana.com/">
More about the Solana blockchain.
</Card>

### Solana Tables

Our Solana data includes the following schemas and tables:

<table><thead><tr><th width="138.00000000000003">Schema</th><th width="302">Description</th><th>Tables</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Raw</td><td>Raw blockchain data for Solana. </td><td>blocks, transactions, instructions, inner_instructions, rewards </td></tr><tr><td>Decoded</td><td>Decoded instructions</td><td>instructions</td></tr><tr><td>Assets</td><td>Token transfers and credit debit of spl-tokens and native SOL. </td><td>transfers, credit_debit, spl_balances, sol_balances</td></tr><tr><td>NFTs</td><td>NFT trades coverage of major marketplaces and NFT mints data.</td><td>trades,  mints</td></tr><tr><td>DEXs</td><td><p>DEX trades and aggregator trades on Solana. </p><p>Coverage includes 10+ major DEXs and multiple protocols. </p></td><td>trades, aggregator_trades</td></tr><tr><td>DeFi</td><td>DeFi related protocols including loans, rewards and farms</td><td>farms, fees_claimed, lending, lending_tvl, lending_tvl_daily, rewards_claimed</td></tr></tbody></table>

### Transaction-level columns
