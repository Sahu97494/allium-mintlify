#### Bitcoin Ecosystem: From Original Chain to Layer 2 Solutions

The Bitcoin ecosystem is a diverse and evolving network centred around Bitcoin, the original and most well-known cryptocurrency.

Over time, the ecosystem has expanded to include various forks and Layer 2 solutions, each aiming to address different challenges and enhance the functionality of the Bitcoin network.
