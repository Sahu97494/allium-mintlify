---
title: "Overview"
---

The defi vertical contains farms and reward claiming information from selector farms on Solana network.

### Farms

`solana.defi.farms`contains information on selected farming entities.

### Rewards Claimed

`solana.defi.rewards_claimed` contains claiming transactions to track the amount of rewards claimed from farming.

### Fees Claimed

`solana.defi.fees_claimed` contains claiming transactions to track the amount of fees claimed from liquidity provision.
