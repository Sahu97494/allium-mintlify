---
title: "Overview"
---

The Move ecosystem is centred around a programming language originally developed for Facebook's Libra/Diem blockchain project.

It's designed for safety, flexibility, and high verifiability in blockchain environments, focusing on asset scarcity and access control.

Move is primarily used for creating layer 1 blockchains with smart contract functionality, such as Sui (developed by Mysten Labs) and Aptos (created by Aptos Labs).
